# Additional Information

The Dungeons is a graphic adventure with some RPG elements viewed from a 1st person perspective as you explore the dungeons looking for a casket containing the secret of the dungeons belonging to a red dragon. 

Before you start your quest you are first shown your ability with various attributes that are strength, wisdom, intellect, constitution and dexterity, with each having a figure between 4 and 20. 

You then select if you are a fighter or a magician before having a choice of equipment to buy with your limited amount of gold coins. The items you can buy include sword, lantern, spells and a suit of armour. Once you are ready with your selection and purchases, it's off into the dungeon.

As you get more gold coins, you can return back to the first room to buy more equipment as you try to find the casket.

In each room of the dungeon, you are able to see any exits and items inside the room and you type in a letter to tell the computer your next action. This can include moving in a certain direction, saying spells, opening items and many others. Creatures also appear and you can attack them before they attack you and weaken or kill you.

You always look in each room in the direction you are moving so for example North can be in a different direction than up.

The objects and monsters are randomly placed at the beginning of each game therefore it is not possible to write a walkthrough, but with below hints you should be able to finish the game.

# Game play

You start in room 1 and have to make it to room 99. Before you go there you have to locate the location with the magic casket. When you open the casket you retrieve a key. 

This key can be used to open the door in room 99 (Look, Open Door while holding the key). You must have a total of at least 1000 points (combining gold coins and experience points). You can see your score if you give the command I (inventory). Move North (east and west are pits) and you've solved the game.

## Choosing your character

At the beginning of the game your abilities are presented. They each have a random value between 4 and 20.

- Strength and Dexterity. If these are high your weapon (attack force) becomes higher.
- Wisdom and intellect. If these are high the chance of a spell succeeding is bigger.
- Constitution. If this is high the chance of being hit by a monster during an attack is lower.

After that you have to choose between Fighter or Magician. A magician can't use armour and a fighter can't use spells.

## Shop

After choosing your character you enter a shop where you can buy some items. You can return to this shop by moving west from the entrance.

## Objects

You can carry 3 objects.

## Spells
- Healing spell. Only for magicians. 	You can recover a random amount of up to 3 hit points.
- Protect spell. Only for magicians. 	You are protected for 12 turns from attacks.

## Food
You need these to prevent starvation. At the start you have 80 stamina points. You have to eat something before they drop to zero.

Eating food adds 50 point to your stamina.

- Iron rations.
- Sandwich pack.

## Armor (only for fighter)
- Shield
- Suit of armour

The suit of armour is the best. If you have the suit of armour you won't need the shield.

## Weapons
- Sword
- Macic Sword
- Flaming Sword

The Flaming Sword is the best, the sword the least strongest weapon.

## Scrolls
If you read scrolls you can use their spell. This can also be done by a fighter.
- Flying scroll 		You can fly one time to another random location.
- Strength scroll	    Read it to get some strength.
- Cursed scroll.		Leave them. If you take them you become cursed and die.

## Bags of gold
Collect as many as you can to reach the 1000 points you need. Each bag contains a random amount between 20 and 120 coins.

## Potions
The effect of drinking a potion is random. You have 7 outcomes:
  1 or 2  your hitpoints increase with a random amount (0-3)
  3 you become protected during 12 turns
  4 You become quicker for a short while during a fight increasing your chance of a succesful attack.
  6 You will lose a random amount of hitpoints (0-2)
  7 You die

As the effect of a potion is random. Save your game before drinking one - just in case.

## Other
- Magic casket.         You have to locate the casket because it contains the key to open the exit of the dungeons.
- Silver mirror.        If you Look while there is a mirror you get scared and you lose a random amount of money.

## Useless items:
- Flask of wine
- Lantern
- Garlic clove
- Knife
- Carrying bag
 
# Commands

Commands are entered by entering the first letter:

S say spell (Health, Protection)
R read Scroll (Flying, Strength)
M move (N,S,E,W)
O open (Casket, Door)
T or G take object
C cut noose (you need a weapon)
A attack monster
F fly after reading a flying scroll. You will fly to a random location between number 9 and 79.
I inventory
H help. Gives you some of the commands
D drop an item
L look (only in room 81 and 99)
P drink potion
Q quit game or save data
X show exits
E eat
B back to next command

# Fighting

| Huge spider |	3 | 10 |
| Giant centipede | 2 |	20 |
| Shrieker | 3 | 30 |
| Purple worm | 6 |	40 |
| Troglodyte | 11 |	50 |
| Red dragon | 9 | 60 |
| Minotaur | 10 | 70 |

Some monsters are placed at the beginning of the game. In room 4 and higher each turn there is a 1/9 chance of encountering a random monster (spider, centipede, worm or shrieker).

* If you are attacked and have protection you won't be hurt, also wearing armour gives a bigger chance of a failing attack.

* The worm and troglodyte have a random chance of emitting poison vapour

* During a fight you may run. If you choose to do this you will run to a room with a number which is one number lower (e.g. from 6 to 5 or from 47 to 46).

## Attacking
The chance of a successful attack is higher if you have a better weapon or if you have used a potion which made you quicker.

## Dying

You can die in the following ways:
- Having a noose around your neck for more than 3 turns.
- Taking a cursed scroll. You become cursed.
- Drinking a potion (see above).
- If your hit points get lower than 1.
- If you fall into a pit (room 99).
- Starvation. Each time you eat you get an extra 50 points.

# Noose

The noose is randomly placed in room 40-49.

If you become trapped in a noose you have three turns to Cut yourself loose with a weapon. After three turns you suffocate.


# Secret door

In room 81 use Look to discover a hidden exit South (the way you came else you can't leave this room).

# Map

Note six rooms on the map are not connected to the rest of the map. You can only reach them by
running from a fight in a room one number higher:

- Run from a fight in room 93 to reach room 92.
- Run from a fight in room 91 to reach room 90.
- Run from a fight in room 86 to return to room 85.
